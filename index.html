    WhatsApp helper: send everything to your own chat in one click

    A website cannot reach into WhatsApp Web by itself, so a small helper extension does it. With the helper, Send all to my WhatsApp opens WhatsApp Web in this browser and posts the text, the pictures and the PDFs, in order, into the chat with yourself ("You", "Message yourself" or your own name). It works for whichever WhatsApp account is logged in to WhatsApp Web here.

    1. and unzip it.
    2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
    3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped report-helper folder.
    4. Log in to WhatsApp Web in this browser, then reload this page.

    Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers on a computer. Phones cannot add extensions: there, each step's Send button opens the share screen, where you pick your own chat.

    The workbook is read, drawn and packed in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is kept after you close the page.
